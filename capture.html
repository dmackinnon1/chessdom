Piece Meal

Piece Meal was inspired by the NYT "Capture" puzzle.

The goal is simple: use the pieces on the board to capture each other until only one piece is left.

Piece Meal puzzles have these simple rules:
  1. The pieces are all the same colour, and must capture each other.
  2. Every move must be one that takes another piece.
  3. Each piece can only make up to two moves.
  4. If there is a king on the board, the king makes the last capture and is the last piece standing.

You can choose to play with a final king or without.

You can select to play with all pieces of the same type (except for the optional final king), or opt for a random mix of piece types.
